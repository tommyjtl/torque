On a laptop use 1234 / QWER / ASDF / ZXCV for these four rows.

  • Sound ○ / T then a pad selects a slot. Factory slots 1–8 play pitches and 9–16 play kit pieces. In any recorded slot, pads 1–16 play consecutive equal slices.
  • Pattern △ / Y then a pad selects a pattern. BPM □ / U selects dial A for tempo. Dial B still controls volume.
  • Write · Enter toggles step editing. Play a note first, then enter Write and tap the steps that should play it. Adding a step while stopped previews its note.
  • Record · top-row × / I arms sampling. All 16 slot LEDs light red. Orange numbers mark saved recordings; choosing one replaces its take. White numbers are factory sounds available for a new take. Press a pad to choose a slot and start the microphone. Its LED blinks during capture. The LCD counts down up to eight seconds and saves automatically. Press Record again to finish early. Allow microphone access when asked.
  • Hold FX (−) during playback and press a numbered pad to apply its effect to the loop. The last pad held takes priority. Release FX or all pads to return to the dry loop. Play · plus or Space starts or stops the loop.
  • Encoder press (O) switches dial. The light above the selected dial shows which one the encoder controls. Play a pad, then turn A to tune that note or drum hit. Each pad in each slot remembers its own pitch for the session. Turn the encoder or press [ / ] to adjust. Turn slowly for fine steps or spin quickly for larger jumps. Press decimal (.) to reset the selected value: pitch to 0 semitones, volume to 50%, or dial A to 96 BPM in BPM mode. On screen, drag a dial up or down, or hover and scroll.

Press Reset or keypad 0 to clear recordings and pattern edits and restore factory settings. Escape stops playback.
